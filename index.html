I see you've reached our website. Hello! We made this website so we can help people who always struggle to fix their own schedule. The planner with a checklist can help people list the things you should do, and you can check the box if it is done or not. We also made a journal to help you write their insights in a day, and reflect the things you should've done that day. And lastly, we made a mood tracker so people can track their emotion in a day. After tracking of everything, you can screenshot of your planner and compare it to your previous activities, and what should be improved.
